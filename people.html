The thing worth knowing

Seven bounties have run since May, across four completely different briefs - a podcast clip, an advert for poidh, a ZABAL Gamez ad, a WaveWarZ stream clip, then three ZAOstock rounds. 5 people entered both an early round and a ZAOstock one: @joeyofdeus, @pascaline, @dee-13, @taku0x, @coolhat. They had no reason to come back except that they wanted to.

This page is not a leaderboard and the order is not a verdict. It sorts by how many bounties someone entered, which is a count of showing up, not a judgement of the work. Somebody with one entry may have made the best thing here. No ranking of quality is published on this site, ever - the same rule the feedback pages are built to enforce.
