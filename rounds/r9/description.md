Make something that gets a stranger to show up at ZAOstock. Best one wins the pot, and the pot grows as others chip in.

ZAOstock is a free music festival on Saturday October 3, 2026, in Ellsworth, Maine. One street, one stage. Franklin Street closes to traffic and eight independent acts play back to back on the parklet stage from noon to six. Free, all ages, rain or shine.

It is a small festival in a small city and the thing it needs is not a logo. It needs people to know it is happening. Twelve days out, that is the whole job.

Take the brand kit, take the lineup, take a photo of the street if you are near it, and make the thing that makes someone say "I will drive to Ellsworth on a Saturday." A clip, a poster, a flyer you actually put in a window, a reel, a photo of the parklet, a 15-second pitch to camera. If you are local, the most useful thing in the world is proof you put something up where a human will see it.


WHY THIS ROUND EXISTS

Because a free festival on one street in a small city lives or dies on whether anyone hears about it, and we would rather pay people who are good at that than shout louder ourselves. The day, the lineup and the times are all public at https://zaostock.com - go and read it before you make anything.


THE BAR (these are requirements, not preferences)

1. Post it publicly and submit the live URL on this bounty page. If it is not claimed here, it is not entered.
2. It has to carry the three facts someone needs to act on: ZAOstock, Saturday October 3, Ellsworth Maine. Free and all ages is a bonus.
3. Tag @bettercallzaal on X and cross-post in the /poidh channel on Farcaster.
4. Use the real brand assets. They are linked below and free to use.
5. AUDIO: if there is audio, use original or one clear instrumental that does not compete with speech. Library music over dialog is a floor fail.


THE RUBRIC (more boxes ticked, more weight)

Reach
+ It went somewhere people who could actually attend will see it - Maine, Downeast, Ellsworth, Bangor, local groups
+ A physical placement photographed as proof beats a post nobody saw
+ Someone who is not in this bounty shared it

Craft
+ Looks finished, not like a draft
+ Vertical or square for mobile
+ Captions if there is speech
+ The date and place are readable in three seconds, without clicking

Substance
+ It says something specific about the day rather than "come to a festival"
+ It names an act from the lineup
+ It is honest about what it is: small, free, outdoors, one street
