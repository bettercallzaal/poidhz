# Winner cast template (Monday) - announce week NN winner + open week NN+1

One cast does both jobs: the announcement and the recruitment are the same
post. This is the single highest-leverage cadence move in the weekly loop - it
compounds instead of restarting cold each week.

Cast AFTER the winner's claim is accepted on-chain (verify the winning post is
still live and public immediately before accepting - acceptance is
irreversible).

```
week <NN> of "this is for the ZAO" goes to <WINNER-HANDLE>.

<ONE-LINE ON WHY IT WON - name the substance beat, not generic praise>

Scored breakdown for every entry: <JUDGING-PAGE-URL>
Winner claim (<PRIZE> ETH on Base): <WINNING-CLAIM-URL>

Week <NN+1> is live right now:
https://poidh.xyz/base/bounty/<NEXT-BOUNTY-ID>

Same format: open your video saying "this is for the ZAO", say something true
about it, post publicly, claim on the poidh page with your post URL. Full
rules + the 100-point rubric in the description. Closes <NEXT-ABSOLUTE-DATE>
11:59pm PT.
```

Checklist:
- [ ] Winner's post verified live BEFORE on-chain acceptance
- [ ] Week NN+1 bounty already cast and escrowed before this goes out
- [ ] Judging page shows the per-entry scored breakdown (it is the appeal
      surface - single judge, published rubric)
- [ ] NFT minted as `ZAO Weekly #<NNN>` (continuous numbering)
- [ ] If runner-up bonuses were promised in the description, send them by hand
      now - poidh pays only the accepted claim
