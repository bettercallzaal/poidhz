MAKE THE ZAOSTOCK POST PEOPLE PASS ON.

Make one piece of media about ZAOstock, post it on any platform, and claim with the one engagement number you want to be judged on. The winner is whoever beats their own usual numbers by the most, so a small account can win.

ZAOstock is a free music festival on Saturday October 3, 2026, in Ellsworth, Maine. Eight independent acts play the Franklin Street parklet from noon to six. Free, all ages, rain or shine. Live at https://zaostock.com/live.


WHY THIS ROUND EXISTS

A free festival on one street lives on whether people pass the word along. A share is somebody putting their own name behind it, which is worth more than a view. So this round pays for the post people actually pass on, not the one that merely gets seen. Everything about the day is at https://zaostock.com.


THE BAR (these are requirements, not preferences)

1. Post it publicly by 5:00pm Eastern, Saturday October 3, and claim here by 5:00pm Eastern, Sunday October 4.
2. Pick ONE stat that means someone passed it on:
   - Instagram: sends or saves
   - TikTok: shares
   - X: reposts plus quote posts
   - Farcaster: recasts plus replies
   - YouTube: shares
   Views and likes do not count. They come too cheaply from autoplay, bots and pods.
3. In your claim, give three things:
   - the stat's number at 24 hours after you posted, with a screenshot
   - your usual number for that same stat, which is the median of your last ten posts
   - the live link
4. Natural only. No paid boosts, no "share to win" giveaways, no engagement pods, no bought anything. A number that does not add up gets a question before it gets paid.
5. The piece carries the facts: ZAOstock, Saturday October 3, Ellsworth Maine, FREE.
6. If you are an agent, say so in your claim.


OPTIONAL: COLLAB WITH US

On Instagram you can send a collab request to @zaofestivals, so the post can appear on both profiles. Accepting is at our discretion, and your stat counts either way.


WHAT SCORES

Your 24-hour number divided by your usual number. Five times your normal on 40 followers beats one times your normal on 40,000. Ties go to the stronger piece.

THE REWARD

Winner takes the whole pot. This is an OPEN bounty, so the pot is whatever this page says right now, and it grows as others contribute. This bounty promises the pot and nothing else.


HOW THE WINNER IS PICKED

I check every claim's numbers after the close and pick the biggest multiple that holds up. Everyone who enters gets a note back, win or lose. I post the pick that week, on Firefly, with the link. Everyone who added to the pot then has two days to vote on it before it pays out.
