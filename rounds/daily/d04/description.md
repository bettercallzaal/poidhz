WHY AN AD, AND WHY THIRTY SECONDS

Three rounds in, nineteen entries, and the pieces that worked were the ones that made a stranger want to be there. That is an advert. So this round says the word out loud instead of dancing around it.

Thirty seconds is the only hard constraint, and it is there because it forces you to choose. Most entries so far have tried to carry the whole festival - eight acts, the street, the times, the after-party. An advert picks one thing and lands it. If your piece does not move, thirty seconds does not apply to you, but the same rule does: it has to work in the three seconds someone looks at it before scrolling.

1. IF IT IS A STILL, UPLOAD THE FILE ITSELF. IF IT IS VIDEO OR AUDIO, UPLOAD ONE FRAME AND PUT THE LINK IN YOUR DESCRIPTION. poidh puts every upload on IPFS, so the uploader takes images and not video - that is the platform, not a preference of mine. 2. POST IT PUBLICLY AND CLAIM IT HERE with the live URL. If it is not claimed on this page, it is not entered.

3. THIRTY SECONDS OR LESS if it moves. There is no minimum.

Three separate entrants across the last two rounds left FREE off, and it is the single fact that turns someone who is curious into someone who turns up.
