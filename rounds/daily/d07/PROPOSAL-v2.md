# Round seven, version two: find new bounty-makers for The ZAO (PROPOSAL, awaiting Zaal)

**Goal, ruled 2026-09-29** (grill item 34, and Zaal's pick in this lane): *new bounty-makers for
The ZAO*, as the first focus. Version one judged the best bounty on paper. That finds good
writers. **A bounty-maker is someone who writes a bounty AND runs it**: they answer entrants,
pick and pay on time, and tell people why. So version two judges the running, not only the
writing.

`description.md` is unchanged until Zaal approves this.

## Two defaults Zaal has not ruled on

| question | default | alternatives |
|---|---|---|
| what winning leads to | **$25, plus an invitation to write and run one ZAO-funded bounty next** | just the $25; top up every good entry |
| what their bounty can be about | **ZAOstock or ZAO Festivals** | any ZAO project |

## How it would work

1. **Create** a poidh bounty about ZAOstock or ZAO Festivals, funded by you from $2, between
   this round's cast and **Sunday October 4, 5pm Eastern**. Claim here with its link.
2. **Give it a deadline no later than Wednesday October 7, 5pm Eastern**, so every entrant
   bounty has finished before this one is judged.
3. **Run it.** Answer questions, pick a winner and pay by your own deadline, and leave every
   entrant a line on why.
4. **This round is picked the week of October 5**, after the Wednesday deadline, on how each
   bounty was written AND run.

## What earns weight

- **Written:** a clear ask in the first line, a stated deadline, proof a stranger can check, and
  an angle that is not already round six.
- **Run:** it drew real entries, questions got answered, it resolved by its own deadline, and
  every entrant got a line back. This half is new, and it is the half that finds a bounty-maker.

## Rules that stay from version one

- Fund it yourself. The size of your pot does not decide this round.
- Canceling your bounty before its deadline withdraws your entry. So does claiming your own
  bounty.
- Agents welcome; say so in the claim.
- Promises: the pot, a note to every entrant, and poidh's contributor vote. With the first
  default, also the invitation.

## What it costs

$25 now, and with the first default one more small ZAO-funded pot for the winner's trial
bounty. The issuer wallet held about $17 on 2026-09-28, so it needs a top-up before this is cast.
