## THE THING THAT IS YOURS TO DECIDE, AND IT IS NOT CLOSE TO OBVIOUS

**@pascaline filed twice, and the cast text judges the wrong one.**

1412's immutable text, line 121: *"If you already claimed, claim again; the later one is the
one judged."*

- **8149** (on-chain 2833) came FIRST, and it is the new drone piece described above.
- **8150** (on-chain 2834) came LATER, and it is **the video she entered in round two** - the
  same X post, dated 22 September, already filed as round-two claim 8115.

Read literally, she claimed twice in round three, so the later claim is her entry, and her
entry is last round's video - which cannot win a round whose whole premise is making something
new. The rule was written to stop someone entering five things and to let people revise.
**Applied here it does the opposite of its purpose: it discards a revision in favour of an
older piece.**

Two honest ways through, and this is a call about fairness, so it is yours:

1. **Judge 8149 and say why out loud.** "The rule says your later claim counts. Yours pointed
   at last round's video, and the earlier one was new work. I judged the new work, because
   that is what the rule was for." Costs nothing, reads as fair, and the reasoning is public.
2. **Honour it literally.** She loses the round with her best piece sitting one claim away,
   and the note says file the new one last next time. Defensible, consistent, and harsh.

**Recommended: option 1**, with the sentence said in the announcement rather than quietly.
This programme's whole credibility problem has been promises that were not kept; a rule bent
in the entrant's favour, explained in public, is a different thing from a promise dropped in
silence.

## THE PLATFORM FINDING THAT CHANGES HOW WE JUDGE EVERYONE

**Kenny, 2026-09-23 16:57 EDT, asked directly:** *"yee man all media is uploaded to ipfs it
would suck if we took videos lol"*

So poidh's uploader is images-only **by design**, because every upload goes to IPFS and video
there is expensive. That closes a question this repo has carried for days and it **reverses a
judgement we have been making**:

**"They filed a screenshot instead of the piece" has been treated as a defect since round one.
It is not one.** Uploading a still and linking the video is the only thing the uploader allows.
Across rounds two and three, **ten of the thirteen claims** went to IPFS as a still because it
is the correct behaviour, and round two's review marked several of them down for it. Measured,
not estimated: the three exceptions are all @assay's.

**But video CAN appear on a bounty page, and one entrant worked out how.** @assay's round-two
claims carry a media url that is not IPFS at all:

- `https://files.catbox.moe/2ypwkq.mp4` - verified live, **13,398,991 bytes**, h264 1080x1920,
  aac audio, 31.5s
- `https://files.catbox.moe/x5x219.mp4` - verified live, **3,328,842 bytes**, same shape, 15.0s

It set the claim's media field to a **direct external mp4** rather than uploading through the
form, and poidh's page plays it. That is why two of fifty-four claims are watchable in place
and both belong to the same entrant.

**UNKNOWN and worth one more question to Kenny:** whether a person using poidh's web form can
supply an external url at all, or whether this is only reachable by calling the contract
directly the way an agent does. If a human can do it, it is the single most useful thing we
could teach entrants. If only an agent can, that is a real and interesting asymmetry rather
than a trick.

**What changes in the next bounty's rules:** stop asking for the file to be uploaded. Ask for a
still plus the link, and tell people the still is what the page will show. We were asking for
something the platform cannot do and then noticing that nobody did it.
