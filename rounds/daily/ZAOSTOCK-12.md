# Twelve days to ZAOstock - a daily bounty ladder

Written 2026-09-20. **Nothing cast.** Twelve daily bounties, 2026-09-21 through 2026-10-02,
every one of them built off https://zaostock.com/brand, ending the day before the festival.

## The improvement to R9, and why it raises reach

**R9 as drafted runs nine days and closes 09-29. Split it: a 24-hour opener tomorrow, then a
daily ladder.** Four reasons, three of them measured.

**1. A 24-hour deadline is the most legible deadline there is, and deadline is the strongest
lever we have.** Bounties with a stated deadline get entries 74% of the time against 48%
without, and the effect survives controlling for format and for prize band - every band
roughly doubles. 81% of the poidh market states no deadline at all. "Closes tomorrow night"
cannot be misread, cannot be bookmarked for later, and cannot be forgotten.

**2. A nine-day window is where intent goes to die.** Nothing in our data measures this, so
it is an argument rather than a finding - but a long window gives the reader permission to
act later, and later competes with everything else in their week. A day does not.

**3. Twelve shots at reach beat one.** One round is one announcement, one share window, one
chance to be seen. Twelve rounds are twelve. And each day's winning asset is the next day's
promo material, so the ladder compounds instead of decaying.

**4. It is cheap enough to be real.** Under-$5 bounties get entries 69% of the time, against
43% for $50-plus. Twelve days at $2 is $24 - less than R3 cost on its own, for twelve times
the surface.

**What it costs us: promise exposure.** Twelve rounds is twelve chances to owe something.
That is survivable only under the daily rule in `rounds/daily/README.md` - **each one promises
the pot and nothing else.** No "we will run it", no "we will pin it". R1 is the only round of
five that kept every promise and it is the only one whose entire commitment was the pot.

## Zaal offered $10/day. Do not spend it as one $10 bounty.

**A $10 bounty lands in the worst-performing band on poidh.** Measured across the 99 live
bounties on 2026-09-20:

| Band | With submissions |
|---|---|
| $2-6 - where a $3 bounty lands | **26/37 = 70%** |
| $6-15 - where a $10 bounty lands | **7/21 = 33%** |

Bounties within $2 of a $10 prize: **2 of 10 got anything**. Doubling the prize from $3 to
$10 does not double entries - on this evidence it halves them. The full curve is not
monotonic and some bands are thin (n=5 at $4-6), but the $6-15 dead zone rests on 21 bounties
across two adjacent bands, which is the most solid negative signal in this dataset.

**So spend $10/day as THREE asks, not one prize:**

| | Per day | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Primary bounty | **$3** | the 70% band, the day's headline ask |
| Second bounty | **$3** | a different shape the same day - doubles the surface, same band |
| Public top-up | **$4** | added to whichever of the two is moving, in public |

Twelve days: **$120 committed, $72 in prizes across 24 bounties, $48 in top-ups.**

**Why the top-up is not just leftover money.** poidhz's whole thesis is that a pot grows
because people want the thing made. Topping up a live bounty mid-day is a reach event in its
own right - "this just doubled, six hours left" is a second reason to post about the same
bounty - and it demonstrates the mechanic we are selling rather than describing it. It is the
only line in this budget that is marketing and proof at the same time.

**What this buys over one $10/day:** 24 bounties instead of 12, all in the band that measures
best, plus 12 public top-up moments. Same money.

Fund it in one go or it will stop halfway, which is worse than never starting.

## Why this order

**Days T-12 to T-9 build the asset library.** Cheap, online, no travel. They produce posters
and clips we can reuse for the rest of the ladder and on the day. Nobody has to be in Maine.

**Days T-8 to T-5 go physical, and this is the part that actually fills a street.** A free
outdoor festival in Ellsworth is won or lost on whether people in Hancock County know it is
happening. A flyer in a laundromat window reaches someone a Farcaster post never will. This
is why the rubric rewards **a photographed physical placement over a post nobody saw** - and
why those days pay the most.

**Days T-4 to T-1 convert.** Awareness becomes attendance only when a specific person is
asked by a specific person. "Invite one person by name" is the highest-intent ask on the
ladder and the cheapest thing to do.

## The rules every day inherits

1. **Promises the pot. Nothing else.** No publication commitment, ever.
2. **Capture-and-show only.** Measured: `irl`, `photo`, `clip`, `video`, `show` get entries;
   `share`, `find`, `help` do not - `help` is three issuers and zero submissions.
3. **24-hour window, stated with a timezone.**
4. **Carries the three facts**: ZAOstock, Saturday October 3, Ellsworth Maine.
5. **OPEN type**, contributor vote, filed in the `wethemmedia` album.
6. **Links the kit**: https://zaostock.com/brand - verified 2026-09-20, the one-click zip is
   live at 12.2MB and every asset referenced resolves.

## Honest risks

**Twelve days is twelve chances to break a promise**, and we currently owe four. The pot-only
rule is what makes that survivable; abandoning the rule mid-ladder is how this goes wrong.

**A local physical ask may get zero entries if nobody in Maine is watching.** The mitigation
is in the order: the first four days are open to anyone anywhere, which is where a Maine
audience would come from in the first place. If T-8 lands with nothing, the ladder is telling
us something true about reach rather than failing.

**$120 is not a marketing budget either, and it should not pretend to be one.** It is a way
to find the handful of people who would have made something anyway, and pay them properly
rather than thanking them. If it works, the output is a reusable asset library and a few
local placements - not a crowd. Judge it on whether anyone in Hancock County sees a flyer,
not on impressions.

**The $10/day recommendation argues against the obvious use of the money, on thin-ish data.**
The $6-15 dead zone rests on 21 bounties and the whole dataset is uncontrolled for how long
each bounty has been open. If the split-into-three approach produces nothing by T-8, try one
$10 prize for a day and compare - that is a cheap experiment and this repo would rather be
corrected by a measurement than keep an argument.
