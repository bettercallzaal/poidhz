MAKE THE BEST RECAP OF ZAOSTOCK 2026.

ZAOstock was Saturday, October 3, 2026, on the Franklin Street Parklet in Ellsworth, Maine: a free music festival, seven independent acts back to back, noon to six. The whole day was filmed, from the drone, from cameras at the stage and on the stream, and all of it is yours to use. Cut it into one vertical video, 90 seconds or under, that makes someone who missed it wish they had been there. Best recap wins the pot.

This closes at 5pm Eastern Sunday, October 11.


WHY THIS ROUND EXISTS

The festival is over and the six hours of it sit in recordings almost nobody has watched. The recordings have about a hundred views between them. One good recap is the thing every act, every partner and every person who was there can share, and it is the piece that tells next year's audience what ZAOstock is. The rounds that handed entrants our own footage and asked for a cut have drawn the most entries this programme has had, so this is that shape again, with the festival itself as the footage: https://zaostock.com

- 09-small-cam-timelapse: 4 time-lapses of the stage. The times in their file names run about three hours fast; the folder's CATALOGUE.md gives the real ones.

THE BAR (these are requirements, not preferences)

1. Vertical, 1080x1920, 90 seconds or under, exported as a video file. It has to be postable as it is.
2. Real footage only: the raw footage folder, the stream recordings, footage you shot yourself, and the brand kit below. No AI-generated crowds, people, stages or performances. These are real artists at a real show.
3. The music is the acts' own. Use their performances from the footage. No library music.
4. Name the acts you show, on screen, spelled the way the lineup page spells them: https://zaostock.com/artists
5. Captions burned in, word for word, for anything spoken.
6. End on the festival: the last frame shows ZAOstock and zaostock.com, readable, for long enough to type.
7. No editor watermark and no corner mark from any tool.
8. UPLOAD ONE FRAME HERE AND PUT TWO LINKS IN YOUR CLAIM. poidh puts uploads on IPFS and takes images, not video. So upload a frame from your recap as the claim image, and in the description give (a) the public post or page where the video plays and (b) a direct download of the video file, open to anyone with the link. Without the file link there is no entry.


GET IT SEEN (optional, never disqualifies you)

- Post it on Instagram as a Collab and invite @zaofestivals as a collaborator. Once accepted, it shows on both profiles and goes to both sets of followers. Invite the acts you feature too, up to five.
- Post it in the /zao channel on Farcaster, where the entries get seen next to each other.


WHAT EARNS WEIGHT

- Someone who has never heard of ZAOstock watches to the end and knows what it was.
- It shows the day, not the stream: the street, the crowd, the stage, the acts.
- Every act gets a moment, or the cut chooses fewer and says why in the claim.
- It works muted, and it sounds good with the sound on.
- The sources in the claim: tell us which files from the folder, or which parts and minutes of the stream, you used.
