WaveZStation is a music site where you buy into a song instead of streaming it: https://wavezstation.com

Go there, find the song with the biggest fan pool that day, buy in for one dollar or more, and record yourself listening to it all the way through and reacting. One take, the whole song. Best reaction wins the pot.

This closes at 11:59pm Eastern Thursday, December 31, 2026.


WHY THIS ROUND EXISTS

Independent artists get heard when one person tells another "listen to this". A reaction video is that, on camera. We want a wall of people hearing these songs for the first time, and we want the artists on WaveZStation to see real listeners, not a play count. The top song changes over the weeks this runs, so the entries will not all be the same song: https://wavezstation.com


THE BAR (these are requirements, not preferences)

1. Buy into the song first, one dollar or more, from your own account on wavezstation.com. Put the share link or the transaction for your buy in the claim.
2. It has to be the top song on the day you record: the one with the biggest fan pool. Take a screenshot of the page that shows it, with the date, and link it in the claim.
3. The video shows you, on camera, listening to the song from its first second to its last, with the song audible. No cuts inside the song. Talk over it, pause it to say something, laugh, whatever is true.
4. Say the song's name and the artist's name out loud, and put both on screen.
5. Real reaction, real person. No AI-generated face or voice, and no reading a script someone else wrote.
6. Post it publicly on X, Farcaster, Instagram, TikTok or YouTube, and tag the artist and @WaveZStation where the platform lets you.
7. One entry per person per song. If the top song changes, you can enter again with the new one.
8. UPLOAD ONE FRAME HERE AND PUT THE LINKS IN YOUR CLAIM. poidh puts uploads on IPFS and takes images, not video. So upload a frame from your video as the claim image, and in the description give (a) the public post where the video plays, (b) your buy, and (c) the screenshot from rule 2. Without the post link there is no entry.


GET IT SEEN (optional, never disqualifies you)
