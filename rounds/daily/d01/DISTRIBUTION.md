## The order, over 24 hours

**1. Cast, then post the announcement within the hour.** A bounty with no post is a bounty
nobody sees. Farcaster /poidh first - it is the room where poidh bounties are expected.

**2. Reply-cast to past submitters, individually, in the same session.** 36 people, minus
femmie. Not a mass tag in one cast - an actual reply or DM that says "you entered R5, this
one is 24 hours and pays $25". This is the highest-leverage hour of the whole day and it is
the thing we have never done.

**3. Maine, and this is the one that matters for the festival.** The entrants who can
photograph a flyer in an Ellsworth window are not on Farcaster. Downeast community groups,
Ellsworth and Bangor local pages, anywhere that takes event posts. A bounty asking for local
placement, posted only to crypto Twitter, will get crypto Twitter answers.

**4. X, same copy, shorter.** Lowest expected yield of the four, included because it costs
one paste.

**5. Mid-window top-up, tomorrow morning.** Add $5-10 to the pot in public and post *that*.
"The pot just went up, six hours left" is a second reason to post about the same bounty, and
it is the mechanic poidhz exists to demonstrate rather than describe.

**6. Two hours before close, one reminder.** Post it at 2pm. "Closes 4pm Eastern, winner
picked live at 5" with the link.

## What to measure afterwards

Write the answer into `rounds/daily/d01/README.md` when it closes, because it decides how the
remaining eleven days are run:

- **claims, and where each came from.** If the reply-casts to past submitters outperform the
  channel post, that changes the default for every future round.
- **did anything come from Maine.** This is the one that matters for the festival and the one
  we have no prior evidence on.
- **did anyone top the pot up** without being asked.

If the answer is that channel posts do nothing and direct replies do everything, that is the
most useful thing this whole run can teach us, and it is worth more than the $25.
