## Farcaster DM (long version)

```
Hey - saw POIDH already ran a bounty for you all a while back (the one Jessebot judged,
timed to a SheFi AMA). Reaching out because we run our own POIDH bounty program at BCZ /
The ZAO (zpoidh, github.com/bettercallzaal/zpoidh) and wanted to pitch something similar.

We've run 4 rounds so far paying real ETH on Base for user-made ads/clips, all public, all
judged against a posted rubric (not a black box). Pitching a round aimed at your community
specifically - an ad/clip bounty for whatever SheFi's promoting right now (a cohort, an
event, a resource), open to your members to submit to. Same structure POIDH used with you
before, just run by us this time, with a full template if useful:
github.com/bettercallzaal/zpoidh/blob/main/docs/P2P-AD-BOUNTY-KIT.md

Why this fits: the bounty's floor requirement is that every submission gets posted
publicly and tagged to your account before anyone even wins - so every entry grows your
reach regardless of outcome, and your members walk away with a real ETH incentive to make
something good, not just goodwill.

What we'd bring: the prize, the description/rubric writeup, and our own open POIDH ops
tooling if useful for anything on your side (fork-ready:
github.com/bettercallzaal/zpoidh/blob/main/docs/PARTNER-GUIDE.md).

What we'd ask: mainly a cross-post/boost to your community when it goes live, so members
actually see it - that's the real distribution value on both sides.

Worth a quick chat about what would actually be useful to promote?
```
