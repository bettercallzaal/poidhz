## The idea being pitched (context for whoever reads this later)

"We Them Media Spotlight" - a POIDH bounty asking the community to make the best
piece celebrating We Them Media (their mission, their story, or a specific creator),
using the exact proven format from [P2P-AD-BOUNTY-KIT.md](../P2P-AD-BOUNTY-KIT.md).
BCZ funds and runs it. Submissions post into the shared `wethemmedia` album - the same
surface BCZ's own rounds already use. Their entire ask is a yes and maybe one repost when
it's done - no weekly cadence, no content production on their end. It's a gift during a
slow stretch, not a favor-ask.
