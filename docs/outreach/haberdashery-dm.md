# The Haberdashery outreach DM - send-ready draft

Grounded in research from [PARTNERSHIP-TARGETS.md](../PARTNERSHIP-TARGETS.md) - The
Haberdashery is already on BCZ's own catalytic-DM whale list
(`docs/how-to-draft-next-bounty.md`, `rounds/_template/cast-templates/catalytic-dm.md`),
and verified live via Warpcast's public API to be a real, active account (`@thehaberdashery`,
Farcaster fid 578265, 791 followers, bio: "A crowdfunded DAO deploying funds to builders
scaling the Base ecosystem"). Separately confirmed via web research: they ran POIDH's
biggest documented bounty to date - a $30K "kickflip" bounty that broke a Guinness World
Record and generated 100K+ views.

This reframes the ask from "DM a whale for a co-funder drop" (BCZ's existing catalytic-DM
pattern, used per-round) into something bigger and more specific: The Haberdashery is a
grants DAO whose entire purpose is funding builders on Base - pitching them a real
co-funded round is inside their actual mandate, not a favor.

Not sent yet - draft for Zaal to review, edit, and send.

## The idea being pitched (context for whoever reads this later)

"ZABAL Big Send" - one audacious, fun, shareable OPEN bounty seeded jointly by BCZ and
The Haberdashery, built for virality rather than utility (the way their own $30K kickflip
bounty was) - a bigger swing than any round BCZ has run solo so far. Uses the same OPEN
bounty co-funding mechanic already proven to work at scale by their own prior bounty.

## DM

```
Hey - we run BCZ/The ZAO's POIDH bounty program (zpoidh, github.com/bettercallzaal/zpoidh).
You're already someone we DM when we cast an OPEN round, for the public co-funder boost -
but this time I wanted to pitch something bigger than the usual ask.

Your $30K kickflip bounty is the clearest proof anywhere that POIDH's OPEN-bounty
co-funding mechanic can actually go viral, not just fund a task. Want to co-seed something
in that spirit with us - a real audacious swing, not our usual clip/ad round? We'd bring
the ZABAL Gamez community and our own seed, you'd bring whatever scale you think makes
sense and the credibility of having actually done this before.

No pressure to match the $30K - even a public co-funder drop the way we normally ask
would be great. But if you're up for actually co-designing something bigger, we're in.

What made the kickflip bounty work, in your experience - worth a quick chat before we
scope anything?
```

## If yes - next steps (for Zaal's reference)

1. Whatever they say about what made the kickflip bounty work should directly shape the
   round design - don't design "ZABAL Big Send" before hearing from them.
2. Decide seed split (BCZ's contribution vs theirs) and who casts the actual bounty.
3. Use [P2P-AD-BOUNTY-KIT.md](../P2P-AD-BOUNTY-KIT.md)'s structure as the base, but this
   round is explicitly NOT a standard ad bounty - THE BAR/RUBRIC may need to flex for
