# Purple DAO outreach DM - send-ready draft

Cold-ish outreach DM pitching a joint POIDH bounty round to Purple DAO. Grounded in
research from [PARTNERSHIP-TARGETS.md](../PARTNERSHIP-TARGETS.md) - Purple funds Farcaster
protocol/ecosystem growth via Rounds.wtf grants and larger on-chain proposals, built on
Nouns Builder, running daily Base auctions for 500+ days. The pitch below is framed
directly against that stated funding thesis, not a generic partnership ask.

Not sent yet - this is a draft for Zaal to review, edit into his own voice if needed, and
send. Pick the Farcaster DM or the shorter X version depending on where you have an actual
contact.

## Who to send this to

Purple DAO doesn't have one obvious single DM target the way a project with a named
founder does - it's a DAO. Realistic entry points: whoever runs their Farcaster
account/channel presence, or a known contributor active in `/purple`. Confirm the right
person before sending rather than guessing - this draft is written to work regardless of
exact recipient.

## Farcaster DM (long version)

```
Hey - running BCZ / The ZAO's POIDH bounty program (zpoidh, github.com/bettercallzaal/zpoidh).
We've run 4 rounds paying real ETH for user-made ads/clips, all on Base, all public and
judged with a posted rubric.

Pitching a joint round: a POIDH bounty asking for ads that promote both ZABAL Gamez (our
build event) and /purple, cross-posted in both channels. Same structure as our last round
- a floor bar (post publicly, tag both accounts, submit the URL), a weighted rubric
(distribution/craft/substance), a public brand kit, and a posted deadline. Full template
is public: github.com/bettercallzaal/zpoidh/blob/main/docs/P2P-AD-BOUNTY-KIT.md

Why this fits what you fund: every valid submission is a public post tagging /purple
before anyone even wins - the bounty pays for content that grows the channel whether or
not it wins. That's the same mechanism a lot of what Rounds.wtf grants for, just funded by
a bounty prize instead of a grant.

What we'd bring: the bounty prize (we've been running ~0.0125 ETH per round), the
description/rubric/brand-kit-template work, and our own POIDH ops tooling (live bounty
dashboard + deadline calendar, all open source if you want to fork it for your own bounty
tracking - github.com/bettercallzaal/zpoidh/blob/main/docs/PARTNER-GUIDE.md).

What we'd ask: co-funding is optional (POIDH's OPEN bounty type lets anyone add to the
pot publicly), but a cross-post/boost from /purple's own reach when it goes live would be
the real value - that's the actual growth mechanism for both of us.

Worth a quick call or just talking it through here? No pressure either way.
```
