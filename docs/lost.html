Work nobody can pay for

225 poidh v2 bounties still read as open and funded, and the contracts holding them are empty. The amount on each one is a bookkeeping field that was never zeroed when the contracts were drained. On 112 of them somebody had already submitted work. That work is still on chain. The money is not.

reading data/stranded.json ...

There is nothing here to claim, and this page exists to say so plainly. Both v2 contracts hold exactly zero, checked against two independent RPC providers. Any figure you see attached to these bounties - including in poidh-app issue #1459, which says they "still hold funds" - is the sum of a struct field, not money. We published that same wrong number ourselves before checking the balance, and poidh's founder corrected us.

So do not spend gas trying to recover it. acceptClaim and cancelOpenBounty cannot move ETH out of a contract with none in it. If you are an issuer, there is no refund waiting. If you entered one of these, the person who owed you cannot pay you from here even if they want to.

What is real is the work. 542 submissions from 323 people are on chain and are not going anywhere. That is the only part of this worth anyone's attention.

Where the numbers come from

Every id on each contract is read directly with bounties(uint256); open means a zero claimer and funded means a nonzero amount. Claims come from getClaimsByBountyId. The scanner is scan-stranded-bounties.py and it runs on the same 6h refresh as the rest of this site. First reported upstream as poidh-app issue #1459, which says these bounties "still hold funds". We confirmed its struct sums to the wei and then found that the contracts themselves hold nothing, so the issue's headline - and our first version of this page - overstated what is there. eth_getBalance is the number that settles it, and it reads zero on both.
