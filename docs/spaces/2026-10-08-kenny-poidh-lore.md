## POIDH lore worth a research note

Numbers are Kenny's words on the call, not verified elsewhere.

- **Traffic:** the peak month was November 2025 at 11,689 unique visitors, after the kickflip world record. About 4,000 in February 2026, a 5,000 plateau from March to July, 9,000 in August and 10,600 in September. Agents now use the contracts directly and do not show up in traffic.
- **The December 2025 hack:** about $500 stolen from about $3,000 in the contracts. A white-hat recovery made every user whole. After it came the bear market and "our main distribution portal gets sold" (Farcaster). Relaunched February 2026.
- **The raise:** one VC would put in $100K if about $300K more came in. That did not happen and the raise is on ice. The summer push still drove the traffic.
- **Funding:** two years of Farcaster grant money have "basically dried up". Bounties were paid forward from grants, not from his pocket. A CEF grant paid for $50 boosts all summer.
- **Big moments this summer:** the zero Chrome extension AI bounty (a couple of thousand dollars). Zoros's 3D-printed blood-powered hardware wallet, claimed by Austin Griffith (Ethereum Foundation). Vitalik adding funds to the local AI Android app bounty "two weekends ago".
- **ZeroDev:** BeFresh Hunter (Arbitrum community lead) put up $100 to "implement zero dev". Kelvin Praises sent a PR within 48 hours, and it merged a few days before the call. A wallet created through POIDH gets its first 5 transactions sponsored.
- **Design:** modelled on Reddit's architecture ("albums" in place of subreddits; poidh.xyz/a/music has 11 past bounties). Only the creator nominates a winner, and contributors can veto and withdraw. "Outcome markets." POIDH is a protocol: poidh.xyz is one front end and poidhz is another, and anyone could build an Apple Pay front end. Kenny himself will not build fiat payments (regulation, part-time).
- **Early lore:** POIDH v1 was Arbitrum-only, with no crowdfunding. In the Degen launch era (December 2023 or January 2024), a crowdfunded, trust-based bounty put a giant knitted purple top hat on the Wall Street bull. Wake put in about $150 and Kenny $50, and the knitter (name uncertain, "Cairo") was paid $200.
- **Community lore:**
  - Eddie (L Junior Diaz), the archetypal user, with Valentine's picnic and appreciation bounties.
  - Ash Money's football shirt bounty: $7, 37 claims, 2025-08-24.
  - The Ethereum-is-for-finance poster on Wall Street, which came from an Edmund Egger tweet.
  - Crypto When Moon's Degen slot machine at Halloween.
  - Jesse Pollak explaining POIDH on his phone at Based House Denver. He still has never created a bounty, the "white whale".
  - Kenji Quest's Farcaster season 1 and 2 video: a $60 bounty from Anoroth with Kenny's boost. It led to Statuette's crowdfund bounty 1411 for Kenji's AI credits, with 17 contributors at the time.
  - Statuette's crowdfunds: Farcon NFC badges (about $1,500) and Ethics Dillon's open neuroscience paper (about $500).
  - The interview-a-politician bounty from the Jen communist, now open to any country.
- **Zaal's practice:** nine ZAOstock bounties, and Kenny named him "best bounty of September". $10 is one "bounty unit", about one a day if he has the bandwidth. The aim is a clipping crew. The minimum bounty is $2.
