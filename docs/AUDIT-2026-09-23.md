# Audit, 2026-09-23 evening - what was wrong and what is fixed

Run while rounds two and three sat unjudged, on an instruction to stop producing and start
checking. **Eleven findings, nine fixed, two left open on purpose.** Every claim below names
the command that produced it.

The theme, and it is uncomfortable: **most of these were us marking other people down for our
own errors, or guards that could not see the failure they were built for.**

## What was actually wrong

### 1. A requirement poidh cannot satisfy, sitting in 24 future bounty drafts - FIXED

Rule 1 of THE BAR read *"UPLOAD THE FILE ITSELF... Attach the video, the audio, the image"*,
under a heading that says **"these are requirements, not preferences"**.

Kenny, asked directly: *"yee man all media is uploaded to ipfs it would suck if we took videos
lol"*. The uploader is images-only by design. **So the first hard requirement of every daily
bounty was impossible for anyone entering with video, which is what rounds two and three both
asked for.**

It was in `rounds/daily/_template/description.md` and in all 24 renderings under
`rounds/daily/generated/`, dated through 2026-10-02. A poidh description is immutable, so
casting any of them would have published an impossible requirement permanently. Round three's
cast text already carries it and cannot be fixed.

Fixed at the template and regenerated: 24 of 24 pass `validate-bounty-description.py`, all
render-safe, and the only diff in every file is that line.

### 2. "Filed a screenshot" was recorded as the programme's dominant failure - FIXED

`rounds/daily/ANALYSIS.md` called it *"the one failure that dominates everything"* and made it
round three's first floor rule. Re-derived per entrant against what each one actually made:

| Round | Genuine miss (a still they could have uploaded) | Not a miss (video, no other option) |
|---|---|---|
| One | 2 of 6 | n/a |
| Two | 1 | 3 |
| Three | 0 | 4 of 4 competing |

What survives is much smaller and still true: a screenshot of your **post** is worse than a
**frame** of your piece, because the bounty page then shows app chrome and a battery icon. That
is one line in someone's feedback, not a floor rule.

### 3. Two entrants were marked down for reading our own site - FIXED

@assay and @barsam were both recorded as stating *"an unpublished closing time"*. The
correction was made on 2026-09-22 and **the wrong version was still live in two files**:
`ANALYSIS.md`'s entrant table, and `d02/REVIEW.md` in a section heading and a recommendation
line. `zaostock.com/program` publishes *"from six (poster: 6 to 10 PM)"*, re-verified today.

### 4. The promise guard could not see the promise we are on record for breaking - FIXED

`postclose-check.py` exists to catch unkept promises. Run against bounty 1409 it reported
**one**. The live text says, twice, *"Live on stream at 5pm Eastern, the same day it closes.
Zaal picks it out loud with the entries on screen."* That stream did not happen and **bounty
1412's cast text apologises for it in writing.**

Its patterns were written from R2 to R5, whose broken promises were pins, publishing and
tokens. The daily run breaks a different shape: first-person commitments, streams, feedback
owed, results posted. Added those. **1409 now surfaces 3 and 1412 surfaces 15.**

Two of 1412's fifteen are noise from the pre-existing `repost` pattern matching rules prose.
Left alone: this is a human sign-off list where a missed promise costs more than two extra
lines, and this repo has already watched one guard get ignored for the opposite reason.

### 5. The same guard read the wrong directory and answered confidently - FIXED

`--bounty 1409 --round 1` read bounty 1409 from chain - which is **daily-01** - then looked for
announcement copy in `rounds/r1`, a different round with a different bounty cast in May, and
printed *"no winner-announce copy anywhere in r1/"* as though it were a finding about round
one of the daily run. The bounty id now decides the folder; an unresolvable one returns None
and says UNKNOWN rather than guessing.

### 6. Nothing checked that our "text as cast" was the text as cast - FIXED, AND TWO WERE NOT

New: `scripts/verify-cast-text.py`, which diffs each round's paste body against what poidh
serves. On its first run, **three of eight cast rounds had no verifiable record and two of the
rest were wrong**:

- **R1 and R2 had no description file at all.** Cast in May, nothing in this repo held what
  they said - so every claim about what they promised, including the unkept ones in
  `PROMISE-AUDIT.md`, rested on one hand-read with no way to check it. Both recovered.
- **R5's file gave the Clippers group as `t.me/wavewarzclipshq`.** What was cast is
  `https://t.me/+U4ZaQ69Qr44wMzRh`. Anyone answering "where do I drop the clip" from this file
  would have sent entrants somewhere they were never sent.
- **Daily 01 held a later, better draft than the one cast** - an added "OPTIONAL, AND GENUINELY
  OPTIONAL" heading and paragraph breaks bounty 1409 does not have. The improvements were real
  and made after casting, so the file was quietly claiming entrants read something they never
  saw.

**Eight of eight now match the chain**, one honest SKIP for R4 which claims nothing.

The script also stopped passing files that claim cast text in prose while having no markers -
r3 and r5 both said "paste-ready" and were being skipped as clean, which is the instrument
being generous about the exact claim it exists to test.

### 7. poidhz.com was telling visitors round three had zero claims - FIXED

It had five. `data/rounds-live.json` regenerates every six hours and was four hours old. On a
ladder of one-day bounties the homepage is therefore wrong about the live round for most of
its life, and the number it is wrong about is the one that says whether anyone turned up.

Back to hourly. The reason it had been dropped to six hours - hourly runs deploying ~25x/day
because the diff check could never pass while `generated_at` churned - **was fixed since by the
`-I '"generated_at"'` guard, and the comment explaining the six-hour cadence outlived the
problem it described.** Verified the guard both ways in a scratch repo: timestamp-only change
suppressed, claim-count change still commits.

### 8. A count guard that refused correct English - FIXED

`check-copy-counts.py` accepted a single bounty's count or the grand total, and nothing else.
Round copy describes **the rounds before this one**, which is neither. It refused "Fourteen
entries from eleven people" (rounds one plus two) and "ten of the thirteen claims" (rounds two
plus three). Worse, the grand total moves while the current round is open, so a true sentence
starts failing the moment someone enters the new one - bounty 1412 going from four claims to
five broke a line nobody touched.

Now accepts any subset sum. **It then immediately earned it**, catching a genuinely stale
number of mine when Kenny's late claim moved the denominator.

### 9. The repo promised a stream the cast text had retracted - FIXED

`rounds/daily/d03/description.md` line 12 said the pick would be *"named live on Twitch"*. The
cast body in the same file says the opposite on purpose. Nothing public was wrong; this is the
line a future session reads first, and repeating a retracted stream promise is how the original
one got made. The cast body was untouched and re-verified byte-identical at 10,264 characters.

## Left open, deliberately

### The `--round` argument on three template and draft rounds

`check-round-copy-agrees.py` fails `rounds/drafts/unlock-solo`, `rounds/drafts/zabal-bugfix`
and `rounds/weekly/_template` because they carry `{{DEADLINE_DATE}}` placeholders and no
"Submissions close" line. All three are templates, none is castable, and the failures are
noise. **Not fixed here because the fix is a judgement about how templates should declare
themselves**, and three noisy lines are cheaper than a wrong exemption rule invented at
speed.

### Whether a human can put video on a poidh page

@assay's claims point their media field at a direct external mp4 -
`files.catbox.moe/2ypwkq.mp4`, verified live at 13,398,991 bytes, h264 1080x1920 with audio -
instead of uploading. Two of fifty-four claims are watchable in place and both are its.

**UNVERIFIED whether a person using poidh's web form can do the same**, or whether it needs a
direct contract call the way an agent makes one. If a human can, it is the most useful thing we
could teach entrants. If only an agent can, that is a real asymmetry and it is interesting.
Until it is answered it must not go into bounty text.

## State of the toolchain

- **26 of 32 scripts have a selftest and 26 of 26 pass.** The six without are all builders or
  renderers: `build-bounty-dashboard`, `prepare-winner-announcement`, `process-judging-videos`,
  `refresh-rounds`, `render-judging-html`, `run-judging-round`.
- `check-external-links.py`: **45 of 45 external links answer**, live and dead controls behaving.
- `check-site-claims.py`: 20 pages, no page invites anyone into a closed round, no dead
  internal link.
- `check-tracked-bounties.py`: every cast bounty tracked, every cast round in `rounds`.
- `verify-cast-text.py`: 8 of 8 cast rounds match the chain.
- `check-copy-counts.py`: 3 of 3 rounds clean.

## The pattern worth keeping

Four of these eleven are the same mistake in different clothes: **we measured what people did,
found a near-universal pattern, and concluded it was a discipline problem in them.** Screenshots
were the platform. The Black Moon time was our own page. The stream was our promise, not their
misreading.

The instrument rule caught one of these live during the audit itself - 24 of 24 drafts
"failing" validation turned out to be a wrong command-line flag, not 24 broken files. That is
the rule working, and it is the reason the other numbers here are worth anything.
